DeepSeek V4 API pricing — V4-Pro and V4.1-Flash, explained

Two live models, a half-price off-peak window, and cache-hit input as low as $0.003 per million. Here are the official rates, what aggregators charge, and how the math compares to Claude.

Official DeepSeek V4 prices (per 1M tokens)

DeepSeek's API currently serves two models: deepseek-v4-pro (DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813) and deepseek-flash (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash). Both take 1M-token contexts with up to 384K output tokens.

The off-peak trick: half price, on a schedule

Peak windows are 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC, Monday–Friday (excluding Chinese public holidays). Everything else — evenings, weekends, and those holidays — bills at exactly 50% of peak rates. A batch job that runs Saturday instead of Wednesday morning costs half as much on the same model. This is unique among major API providers; Claude and Gemini price flat around the clock.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the DeepSeek V4 API cost?
Peak per 1M tokens: V4-Pro is $1.32 input (cache miss) / $3.96 output; V4.1-Flash is $0.30 / $1.20. Cache-hit input drops to $0.044 (Pro) and $0.006 (Flash), and off-peak hours halve everything.
What are DeepSeek's off-peak hours?
All hours except 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC Mon–Fri (excluding Chinese public holidays). Weekends are always off-peak, and off-peak rates are exactly half of peak.
Is DeepSeek V4 cheaper than Claude?
At the frontier tier, yes: V4-Pro output is $1.98–$3.96 vs Opus 5.5's $20 (~5–10x cheaper). At the small-model tier Claude Haiku 5.5 ($0.10/$0.50) actually undercuts DeepSeek Flash at list price — DeepSeek wins on context length and off-peak discounts instead. See the full comparison.
What happened to DeepSeek V4-Flash?
Retired. The old deepseek-v4-flash and deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp names still work but route to DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at the same Flash pricing — no code change needed, and vision support is retained on Flash.
Is there a DeepSeek V4 free tier?
The official API has no free tier, but third-party gateways do: Vercel AI Gateway includes $5 of free credits every 30 days, and OpenRouter's playground lets you test models without a commitment. DeepSeek's own chat app is free to use.
