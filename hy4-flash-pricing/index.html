FAQ

How much does Hy4 flash cost?

$0.07 per million input tokens and $0.28 per million output tokens at list (model ID hy4-flash), with cache reads at $0.003/MTok, verified Nov 17, 2026 against Tencent's announcement and the OpenRouter model page. The 50% TokenHub promo runs it at $0.035/$0.1405 through Nov 30, 2026.

How long does the 50% discount last?

Through Nov 30, 2026. The OpenRouter Tencent Cloud provider was already billing the promo rate ($0.035/$0.1405) on Nov 17 — exactly half of list. From Dec 1 the full $0.07/$0.28 applies, so treat the promo as a one-month migration discount, not the steady-state price.

Is Hy4 flash actually as good as Claude Haiku 5.5?

Tencent's launch claim is blind-test parity with Haiku 5.5 at ~18x lower task cost. That's a vendor claim about Tencent's own evals — treat it as directional, and settle it with a side-by-side on your workload, which costs pennies at these rates.

How does Hy4 flash compare to Gemini 4 Hydrogen?

On price, it's not close: ~21x cheaper input, ~27x cheaper output, both flat to 1M context — the same agent shape that bills ~$102/month on Hy4 flash lists at ~$2,273/month on Hydrogen. Hydrogen is Google's fast agentic tier with much higher measured throughput and launch-day benchmark scores — pay the ~22x premium only when latency and reasoning depth demand it. For background classification, extraction, and high-volume summarization, Hy4 flash's price line is the story.

Does the price go up on long contexts?

No long-context surcharge was published at launch — the $0.07/$0.28 pair covers the full 1M-token window. Cache reads at $0.003/MTok apply when your agent re-reads the same context across runs.
