Liquid AI's decision models (launched Nov 16, 2026) price d1.2 at $0.02 per MTok of input and d1.2-ultra at $0.042 — with no output line at all, because they return calibrated answers with zero generated tokens. Benchmarks at launch — and their fine print

  • Decision Index v2.1: d1.2-ultra scores 75 — #1 among decision models, two points ahead of the previous best, per Artificial Analysis' index as reported by Liquid; no model beats d1.2 for less money on that index.
  • Calibration error across 13 benchmarks (~22,505 questions): d1.2-ultra 0.8, d1.2 1.2 — vs 2.7 for Jev 1.14 and 7.2 for GPT-6 Luna per Liquid's table. Low calibration error means the confidence scores can be trusted for routing.
  • Internal task benchmarks: receipt value reading 98% (ultra) / 96% (d1.2); RefCOCO grounding 94% both; audio QA 95-97% on ESC-50 and FLEURS across 43 languages.
  • The fine print: the five new answer types (values, spans, boxes, ranges, multi-select) have no public benchmarks — those numbers come from Liquid's own held-out sets. Pilot on your data before trusting the 98s.
Price positioning: d1.2 is the cheapest per-question model on this site — several-fold under the cheapest closed-set competitor and ~19x under a generated-answer Flash-tier model on the same shape — and d1.2-ultra buys the #1 decision-model ranking for about one-fifth of a cent per 50 questions. Against Gemini Decision 1 the trade is simple: 5x cheaper input and a 65,536-token per-question window vs Decision 1's once-per-request batch billing. Against generated-answer LLMs there is no comparison on price — the question is only whether closed-set answers fit your task. For document extraction, audio QA, and routing at volume, the answer is increasingly yes.

FAQ

How much does Liquid d1.2 cost?

$0.02 per million input tokens for d1.2 and $0.042 for d1.2-ultra, billed input-only, verified Nov 17, 2026 against Liquid's launch post, official docs, and the OpenRouter listing. Output is always $0 — the models emit zero generated tokens.

Why is there no output price?

The models don't generate text. They return calibrated answers — a value, span, bounding box, numeric range, or multi-select — with a confidence score, so usage.output_tokens is always 0. Since generation is the expensive half of most LLM bills, removing it collapses per-question cost.

How does d1.2 compare to Gemini Decision 1?

5x cheaper input for d1.2 ($0.02 vs $0.10), ~2.4x for d1.2-ultra. Both are closed-set answer engines. The structural difference: Decision 1 bills input once across up to 1,000 questions in a single request, while Liquid bills state once per request plus each question's own tokens — for batch-heavy shapes, read our Decision 1 page before choosing.

What are the input limits?

65,536 tokens per question including images and audio, with a 250,000-byte text cap per question. Media pricing: images ~1.5 tokens per 32×32 patch (1024×1024 ≈ 1,536 tokens), audio 10 tokens per second. Shared context bills once per request, not per question.

Is d1.2 on OpenRouter?

Yes — live at the same input-only rates as of Nov 17, 2026, alongside the day-one Liquid API. Vercel support was also announced at launch. Confirm provider coverage before routing production traffic.
