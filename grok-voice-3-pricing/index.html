Grok Voice 3.0 API pricing — $0.08/min speech-to-speech, TTS and STT rates

xAI's Voice API bills the simplest way in realtime speech: a flat $0.08 per minute ($4.80/hr) for speech-to-speech on grok-voice-3.0, plus $0.004 per text input; $0.10–0.20/hr for speech-to-text and $15 per 1M characters for text-to-speech. Every rate below is from xAI's published pricing table.

What a voice agent actually costs per hour

Flat per-minute pricing makes the arithmetic boring, which is the point. An hour-long speech-to-speech session is $4.80 plus text-input surcharges; a support line handling 40 hours of calls a day budgets to $192/day before telephony. On the other endpoints, transcribing 10 hours of audio is $1.00 on REST or $2.00 streaming, and narrating a 40,000-character script at TTS rates costs $0.60. Note the asymmetry: STT is priced per hour, S2S per minute, TTS per character — three units in one API.

Where Grok Voice fits next to Claude

Claude has no speech endpoints — the production pattern is a realtime voice layer for the conversation and a text model for the heavy reasoning. A Grok Voice agent that hands structured call summaries to Claude Haiku 5.5 ($0.10/$0.50 per MTok) pays $4.80 per call-hour for the voice plus fractions of a cent for the follow-up reasoning; for the Claude-side math use the token calculator. If your workload is recorded-audio transcription rather than live calls, Grok's STT at $0.10/hr competes with Voxtral's $0.0035–0.0040/min batch ($0.21–0.24/hr) — Mistral is dramatically cheaper for files, Grok's flat rate is about the live loop.

FAQ

How much does the Grok Voice 3.0 API cost?

Speech-to-speech is $0.08/min ($4.80/hr) plus $0.004 per text input; speech-to-text is $0.10/hr REST or $0.20/hr streaming; text-to-speech is $15 per 1M characters. Verified Nov 9, 2026 against xAI's published pricing table.

Is Grok Voice billed per token?

No — per minute for speech-to-speech, per hour for speech-to-text, per million characters for TTS. The only per-unit surcharge is the $0.004 text-input charge in xAI's table. That makes it the easiest realtime voice budget in the current field, where OpenAI and Google bill audio as tokens.

What happened to grok-voice-2.0?

It's deprecated at the same $0.08/min rate. Migrating to grok-voice-3.0 is a model-name change, not a repricing event.

Is Grok Voice 3.0 the best speech-to-speech model?

It launched Oct 14, 2026 topping third-party speech-to-speech indexes and xAI cites the #1 spot on the Tau Voice Leaderboard — strong starting evidence, but leaderboards move monthly. Run your own bake: sub-second latency and 25+ languages are the spec-sheet claims that matter for agent deployments; test accents, tool-use turns and barge-in on your traffic before committing.

Cheapest way to transcribe with xAI?

The REST speech-to-text path at $0.10 per hour. For file-transcription-heavy workloads, Mistral's Voxtral batch ($0.21–0.24/hr equivalent) undercuts it substantially — the two APIs win different jobs.
