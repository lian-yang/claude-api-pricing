The angle: frontier-tier ARC scores at small-model rates

ARC Prize published Inkling 2 Small's verified results in mid-November 2026: 81.7% on ARC-AGI-2 (Verified), with ARC-AGI-1 and ARC-AGI-3 results published alongside. ARC-AGI-2 is the abstraction benchmark where frontier models historically scored single digits to low tens — an 81.7% at $0.30/$1.00 is the strongest reasoning-per-dollar datapoint of this launch cycle. The catch is speed: this is a reasoning model, and time-to-first-answer runs 12-38 seconds depending on provider.

Launch facts that matter for billing

  • Uniform rates, real differences: all four providers list $0.30/$1.00, but blended cost, per-task cost, TTFT, and error rates differ enough to matter — Together is the fast lane (10.5 min per II task, 12.6s TTFT), DeepInfra the cheap lane.
  • Cache is the discount lever: cache hits carry a significant discount vs fresh input — the reason the blended price ($0.18) sits ~40% under list. Cache write/storage bill separately per provider.
  • Open weights: self-hosting is the floor-cost option; four hosted providers already carry it at identical rates.
  • Reliability datapoint: Thinking Machines' first-party endpoint measured a 0.0% error rate on AA's runs — relevant for unattended pipelines.

FAQ

How much does Inkling 2 Small cost?

$0.30 per million input tokens and $1.00 per million output tokens on DeepInfra, Nebius, Together AI, and Thinking Machines' own API, verified Nov 17, 2026 against Artificial Analysis' provider table. Blended with cache hits at AA's 7:2:1 weighting it runs ~$0.18 per MTok.

Why do providers cost differently if rates are identical?

List price is uniform but realized cost isn't: blended prices run $0.18-$0.20 and cost per Intelligence Index task runs $1.15-$2.01 across the four providers. Latency differs most — 12.6s vs 37.9s time-to-first-answer. Choose by latency and reliability, not the rate card.

How does the price compare to frontier models?

~3x under GPT-6.1 Terra's input, ~7x under Sonnet 5.5's, 10x under Kimi K3's — at an ARC-AGI-2 (Verified) score of 81.7% published by ARC Prize in mid-November 2026. If your workload is abstraction-heavy rather than long-context or latency-sensitive, the price-performance gap is the largest in the current lineup.

Is there a free tier?

No free hosted tier appears across the four tracked providers. The model is open-weights, so self-hosting is the cost floor if you have GPU capacity.

What is the context window?

262K tokens on Thinking Machines' endpoint. Other providers' context limits weren't rendered in AA's comparison table on Nov 17 — verify on each provider's model card before long-context commitments.
