HeyGen Avatar 6 pricing — per-second API rates and plan credits

Avatar 6 is HeyGen's newest talking-avatar engine. It has two price tags: per-second API rates ($0.05–$0.10/sec) and in-app credits (20–58 per minute). The gap between them is the whole story — credits render the same video for about a third of the API price.

Turbo vs Pro — how to pick

HeyGen's help center names the two Avatar 6 engines Turbo (20 credits/min) and Pro (40 credits/min); OpenRouter's listings carry the same 2x spacing ($0.05 vs $0.10 per second). HeyGen doesn't publish an official mapping between the API listing names and the in-app engine names, so confirm which engine a given API listing renders before you commit a pipeline to it. Rule of thumb: Turbo when turnaround and cost dominate, Pro when render quality is the deliverable. At 2K the credit gap narrows to 35 vs 58 per minute.

How that compares with other AI video

  • Kling 3.0 bills per second in credits too — ~6–12 credits/s at 720p–1080p, so a 5-second clip lands near $0.68 at list; Avatar 6's credit math is closer to Kling's than to token-based LLM pricing
  • Audio-side APIs run per-minute like Avatar 6 credits: see Voxtral API pricing and Grok Voice 3
  • Token-based model APIs don't map to per-second video at all — that's why this page keeps both columns in minutes and seconds, not MTok

Frequently asked questions

How much does HeyGen Avatar 6 cost per second?
Through API aggregators, from $0.05/sec (Turbo) and from $0.10/sec (standard listing), charged by actual seconds generated. The HeyGen app itself doesn't bill per second — it draws 20–58 credits per minute.
How much is Avatar 6 per minute on credits?
Turbo: 20 credits/min at 720p/1080p, 35/min at 2K. Pro: 40/min at 720p/1080p, 58/min at 2K. At the $0.05 marginal credit rate that's $1.00–$2.90 per minute.
Credits or API — which is cheaper?
Credits, by about 3x at the margin: Turbo ≈ $1.00/min vs $3.00/min API; Pro ≈ $2.00 vs $6.00. The API premium buys programmatic access, webhooks and batching — for manual creation, credits win.
What do HeyGen plans cost?
Creator $29/mo with 600 credits, Pro from $49/mo with 1,000+ credits, Business $149/mo with 1,500 credits and $0.05/credit top-ups in $5 blocks. A Free plan exists for testing.
Turbo vs Pro — which should I use?
Pro draws exactly twice Turbo's credits at 720p/1080p and costs twice as much per second via API. Turbo when cost and turnaround dominate; Pro when render quality is the deliverable.
How much is a 3-minute Avatar 6 video?
60 credits on Turbo ≈ $3.00, or 120 credits on Pro ≈ $6.00, at 720p/1080p via credits. Same render via API: $9.00 (Turbo) or $18.00 (standard listing). Add 10–20% for retries.
