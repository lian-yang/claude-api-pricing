Gemini 4 Argon API pricing — and the Dec 1 price increase

Google's flagship reasoning model bills $2 / $10 per 1M tokens (input / output) as an introductory rate through November 30, 2026 — then doubles to $4 / $20. Here's the full rate card, what a real workload costs before and after, and the two levers (batch, caching) that cut the bill.

The Gemini 4 Argon rate card

Argon (gemini-4-argon) went global on October 13, 2026 with a ~1M-token context window (shared across input, thinking and output). It is paid-tier only — no free tier, unlike the Flash models. Every price below doubles on December 1, 2026:

The caching lever, concretely

Agents that resend a long system prompt on every call pay full input price for tokens the model has already seen. Argon's implicit caching bills those repeats at $0.10 per 1M tokens — 95% off the $2 fresh rate (95% off still holds after Dec 1: $0.20 vs $4). A support-agent workload resending a 50K-token system prompt 1,000 times a day pays $100/day at fresh rates, but $5/day once the prompt is served from cache. No configuration is needed — the API reuses cached prefixes automatically — but only exact shared prefixes qualify, so put stable content first and per-request content last.

Does the Gemini API key itself cost money?

No — keys are free and you pay per token. On Flash models a free tier covers experiments; on Argon you need a billing-enabled project before the first call. Third-party aggregators (OpenRouter and similar) resell Argon at their own per-token rates — worth a look for testing, but for production the official Batch + caching stack is usually the cheapest compliant path.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Gemini 4 Argon API cost?
$2 per 1M input and $10 per 1M output tokens (output includes thinking tokens) through November 30, 2026; $4 / $20 from December 1. Cached input is $0.10 per 1M until Nov 30, then $0.20. No free tier.
Does the intro price really double on December 1?
Yes — Google's rate card lists $2 / $10 as introductory through Nov 30, 2026 and $4 / $20 from Dec 1, a straight 2x on input and output. Batch and caching double on the same date.
Is there a free tier for Gemini 4 Argon?
No — Argon is paid-only, unlike Flash models. The key is free to create but calls need a billing account. Cheapest paid path is Batch at half rate.
How do I make Argon cheaper?
Batch API: 50% off ($1 / $5 intro, $2 / $10 after) for jobs tolerating up-to-24-hour completion. Implicit caching: repeated input prefixes at $0.10 / 1M — 95% off fresh input. Together they compound: a cached-prefix batch call runs at roughly 2.5% of the fresh standard input rate.
Argon vs Claude Opus 5.5 — which costs more?
Intro Argon is half of Opus ($2 / $10 vs $4 / $20). From Dec 1 they're identical at $4 / $20; the tiebreaker becomes context (1M vs 200K) and ecosystem. On the 30M+5M daily workload above, that's $3,300 vs $6,600/month before Dec 1, then $6,600 vs $6,600.
Argon vs Gemini 3.8 Flash — what's the gap?
About 2.7x on both input and output, and the ratio survives both price increases (Argon Dec 1, Flash Jan 1, 2027). Flash keeps a free tier. Default to Flash for volume and latency; pay the 2.7x when reasoning depth is the deliverable.
Does a Gemini API key cost money?
The key is free; you pay per token. Argon requires a billing-enabled project (no free tier). Google Search/Maps grounding adds 5,000 free requests/month across Gemini 3+ models, then $14 per 1,000.
