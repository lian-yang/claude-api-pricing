GPT-6.1 Sol vs GPT-6 Astra — the 1/5 model

OpenAI's own positioning: Sol nearly matches Astra on agentic coding, computer use and professional work. The bill says the rest — $2/$10 vs $10/$50 per million tokens, exactly one-fifth at every tier, batch included. Where the money goes, with sliders.

Read the table as one number: 5. Every Sol price is exactly one-fifth of the matching Astra price — standard, long-context, batch and even the Fast/Ultrafast latency tiers (Sol $4/$20 fast, Astra $20/$100). The 272K long-context surcharge adds 50% to output on both sides, so it doesn't move the ratio either. The two ratios that do differ: caching (Astra's cached input is 10x Sol's) and the GPT-6 Luna small tier ($0.10/$0.50) if your task fits a 20x-cheaper model.

Where each model earns its keep

  • Sol's case: near-Astra agentic coding and computer use at 1/5 the rate card, faster Codex completion (~3x per The New Stack testing), batch/flex halving it again, and the cheapest route to GPT-6-class reasoning short of Luna ($0.10/$0.50) for tasks that fit a small model.
  • Astra's case: the flagship tier for a reason — peak-velocity computer-use sessions, the last few points of SWE-bench-style evals, priority rate limits. If your monthly token bill is small, 5x on a $50 base is noise; at $4,500/mo it's a headcount.
  • The honest tiebreak: run your own 20-task eval on both. The price math is settled (80% saved); only your workload decides whether Astra's residual edge is worth it. For the cross-vendor version of this comparison, see GPT-6.1 Sol vs Opus 5.5.

Frequently asked questions

Is GPT-6.1 Sol as capable as GPT-6 Astra?
Per OpenAI, Sol nearly matches Astra on agentic coding, computer use and professional work, and runs about 3x faster on Codex. Independent testing measured the same accuracy at roughly 18-20% of Astra's cost. Astra keeps the edge on the hardest agentic runs — but capability-per-dollar belongs to Sol for most workloads.
Why is GPT-6.1 Sol so much cheaper than Astra?
Deliberate tier positioning, not a promo: Sol is the workhorse tier ($2/$10 short-context) under Astra's flagship ($10/$50). The 5x gap is structural — it holds across standard, batch, fast and ultrafast rates, so no volume discount closes it.
Does the 1/5 gap change for long context or batch?
No. Past 272K input both step up (Sol $4/$15, Astra $20/$75) and the ratio holds. Batch/Flex halves both equally (Sol $1/$5, Astra $5/$25). Only caching breaks symmetry: Astra's $1.00 cached input is 10x Sol's $0.10, so cache-heavy pipelines narrow the effective gap on the input side.
When is Astra still worth 5x?
Hardest computer-use sessions where a failed run costs more than tokens; evals where Astra clears a bar Sol misses; priority rate limits at peak. Everything else belongs on Sol. Note this page compares OpenAI's own tiers — not to be confused with the retired GPT-5.6 Sol SKU at $4/$20.
