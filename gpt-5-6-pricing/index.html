GPT-5.6 API pricing — Sol, Terra and Luna, explained

Three tiers from $0.20/$1.20 to $4/$20 per million tokens, a promotional deadline on Sol (Nov 21), and a value story that outlived the GPT-6 launch. Here are the official rates and how they compare to Claude.

Official GPT-5.6 prices (per 1M tokens)

OpenAI announced the GPT-5.6 family on July 30, 2026 ("Advancing the price-performance frontier") with three tiers. In October 2026 they sit one rung below the new GPT-6 flagship line — which is exactly why they're the value pick.

ModelInputOutputPositioning
GPT-5.6 Sol$4.00$20.00Flagship tier; promo rate at least through Nov 21, 2026
GPT-5.6 Terra$2.00$12.00Balanced default
GPT-5.6 Luna$0.20$1.20High-volume workhorse (cut ~80% in 2026)

Source: OpenAI pricing page & model docs, verified Oct 17, 2026. Sol originally launched at $5/$30 and was cut ~20% input / ~33% output during the promotional window.

The Nov 21 deadline on Sol

Sol's $4/$20 is explicitly promotional, available at least through November 21, 2026. OpenAI hasn't published what comes after. If your product runs on Sol, budget against the original $5/$30 or plan a fallback to Terra ($2/$12) for the workload share that tolerates it — the same deadline-style math we track for Gemini 4 Argon's Dec 1 doubling.

GPT-5.6 vs Claude, honestly

Terra vs Sonnet 5.5: same $2 input, Terra pays $2/M more on output ($12 vs $10) — you're paying a small premium for OpenAI's frontier-tier training run at the mid tier. Sol vs Opus 5.5: Sol matches Opus's $20 output and undercuts it on input. Luna vs everything: $1.20 output is ~8x cheaper than Sonnet and ~17x cheaper than Opus — if Luna's quality clears your bar, the others don't get a look-in on volume work.

After GPT-6: the value-tier case

GPT-6 Astra is the new flagship at $10/$50. That repositioned 5.6 rather than retiring it: Terra and Luna are now OpenAI's price-performance line, the same slot DeepSeek V4 occupies from the other side — see our DeepSeek V4 pricing for the budget anchor and Kimi K3 for the $3/$15 challenger in the same ballpark.
