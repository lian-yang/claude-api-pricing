App vs app: the free-tier tiebreak

If you're comparing the consumer apps, it's close: both Meta's Muse Spark app and the Claude app are freemium products with usable free tiers and ~$20/month Pro plans. The structural difference is underneath — Muse Spark's engine ships as open weights (released Oct 15, 2026), so third parties route it through aggregators like OpenRouter and anyone can self-host. Claude is closed and API-only. For a developer, "Muse Spark vs Claude" is really open-weights economics vs the Claude API — which is the math below.

The self-host reality check

"Free weights" is not free inference. Muse Spark Open is an 816B-parameter mixture-of-experts with ~42B active per token — the sparsity is why Meta claims it runs on high-end Macs, but you're still looking at a 512GB-class unified-memory machine or a multi-GPU server. Renting that hardware runs $15–25/hour, which at low-to-moderate volume exceeds the $1.25/$4.25 hosted price many times over. Self-hosting only wins at sustained high throughput, data-sovereignty requirements, or heavy prompt reuse — for everyone else, the aggregator price already captures most of the open-weights discount.

The context-window multiplier

Muse Spark Open carries the same 1M-token window as DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3 — about 5x Opus 5.5's ~200K. A task needing 600K of context on Claude means three requests and three overlapping-context bills; on Muse Spark it's one. Claude's mitigations are prompt caching (cached Opus input ~$0.20/M) and better per-attempt quality. Whole-repo review and massive document QA still favor the big-window models on raw economics.

Where Claude still earns the 3–5x

  • Coding per attempt: Opus 5 tops the SWE-bench Verified leaderboard; benchlm's head-to-head has Opus 5.5 at ~86.6% vs Muse Spark ~60.5%, and Opus leads SWE-Bench Pro 69.2% vs 61.5% (Muse Spark 1.1)
  • But the gap is closing: Muse Spark 1.3 edges Opus 5 on the community DeepSWE board (75.4% vs 74.0%), and Muse Spark 1.2 posts 86.6% on SWE-bench Verified at $0.55–$1.25 per solved task — the cost-per-solved-task number is where open weights bite
  • Agentic maturity: Claude Code, computer use, prompt caching and batch APIs are battle-tested; Muse Spark's tooling is weeks old
  • Retry math: if the cheaper model needs 2–3x more attempts on your workload, the 4.7x output discount evaporates — benchmark both before routing
Practical split: route bulk, long-context, retry-tolerant work (summarization, extraction, code analysis, batch agents) to Muse Spark Open at $1.25/$4.25 — a ~70% cut versus Opus 5.5 at list. Keep Opus 5.5 for the frontier slice where success per attempt matters: hard agentic chains, delicate refactors, high-stakes writing. That's the same 90/10 split we recommend in every open-weight comparison — Muse Spark just moves the bulk tier's price closer to DeepSeek territory with a 1M window attached.

Frequently asked questions

Is Muse Spark cheaper than Claude?
At list API prices, yes: ~$1.25/$4.25 per million tokens via OpenRouter vs Opus 5.5's $4/$20 — about 3x cheaper on input, 4.7x on output. Versus Sonnet 5.5 ($2/$10) it's ~1.6x/2.4x. Self-hosting drops the token price to zero but only after hardware that can serve an 816B MoE.
Is the Muse Spark app free like the Claude app?
Both have free tiers and ~$20/month Pro plans — as apps they're similar freemium products. The real difference is structural: Muse Spark's engine is open-weight (self-host or aggregator routing possible), Claude's is closed and API-only.
Can Muse Spark Open run locally on a Mac?
Meta claims Mac feasibility thanks to the sparse 42B-active MoE design, and quantized community guides exist — but realistically you want a 512GB-class unified-memory machine or multi-GPU server. At $15–25/hour for that hardware, most developers are better off paying $1.25/$4.25 hosted.
How does Muse Spark compare to Claude on coding benchmarks?
Opus 5 leads SWE-bench Verified overall; Opus 5.5 leads Muse Spark ~86.6% vs ~60.5% on benchlm's head-to-head, and Opus wins SWE-Bench Pro 69.2% vs 61.5%. Muse Spark 1.3 edges Opus 5 on community DeepSWE (75.4% vs 74.0%), and Muse Spark 1.2 hits 86.6% on SWE-bench Verified at $0.55–$1.25 per solved task. Claude wins per attempt; Muse Spark wins per dollar.
How big is Muse Spark's context window vs Claude's?
1M tokens vs Opus 5.5's ~200K — roughly 5x. Long-context jobs avoid being split into multiple requests, saving both latency and repeated-context token fees. Claude's counterweights: prompt caching and stronger per-attempt quality.
When is paying for Claude still worth it?
When capability per attempt dominates: long-horizon agentic work, delicate refactors, high-stakes writing — plus caching and batch discounts that cut Claude's effective price for heavy users. Run both on your real workload before routing; retry rates decide the math.
