FAQ

How much does Qwen4 Max cost?

$1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens (model ID qwen4-max), verified Nov 17, 2026 against launch coverage, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio pricing, and the OpenRouter model page. Pricing is flat across the full 1M-token context; cache reads are $0.075/MTok; the OpenRouter provider rate was observed at $1.425/$4.275.

Is Qwen4 Max cheaper than Qwen 3.8 Max?

Yes — $1.50/$4.50 vs 3.8 Max's $2/$6, a 25% cut on both lines, alongside claimed score gains. Alibaba's launch framing pitches roughly 60-62% lower task costs than competitors; treat vendor task-cost math as directional and rerun it on your own workload shape.

Does the price go up on long contexts?

No. Qwen4 Max publishes one rate pair for the entire 1M-token window — no surcharge tiers like GPT-6.1 Terra's 2x beyond 272K. Long-context agent runs are where the flat window saves the most.

Is there a free tier or batch pricing for Qwen4 Max?

Launch pricing material published neither a free API tier nor a batch variant. Cache reads at $0.075/MTok are the main published discount lever; QwenCloud subscriptions are the other. Check Alibaba Cloud's pricing page for region-specific rates before committing.
